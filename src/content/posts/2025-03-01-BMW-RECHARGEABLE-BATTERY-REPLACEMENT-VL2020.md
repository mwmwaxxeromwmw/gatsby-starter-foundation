**Symptom: BMW E90 Remote key buttons completely unresponsive**

* **1. Diagnostic Assessment**
Before performing any invasive disassembly, we verified the issue using a dedicated RF signal tester. The key failed to emit a signal on any button press.

* **2. Internal Inspection**
Unlike standard remotes with snap-in batteries, the non-comfort access BMW E90 key fob uses a sealed, soldered rechargeable cell (Panasonic VL2020). We opened the casing and measured the circuit voltage, which had dropped below 2V—well under the operating threshold.

* **3. Component Repair**
We carefully desoldered the exhausted cell from the circuit board without damaging the adjacent micro-components. 

After soldering in a fresh replacement battery, we retested the frequency output, every button responded instantly with full signal strength, returning a fully working key to a satisfied client at a fraction of the cost of a dealership replacement.
